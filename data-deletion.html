User data deletion

Last updated: 10 November 2026 (Africa/Accra, GMT)

This page explains how to ask Catalpa SI to delete personal data we hold in connection with the WhatsApp ordering bots we operate for client businesses, and in connection with this website. It is the user data deletion instructions URL we provide to Meta. The full Privacy Policy describes what we collect and why.

How to request deletion

You can request deletion in either of these ways:

  1. Email p.samuelamoakoh@gmail.com with the subject line Delete my data. Please include the WhatsApp number you used and the name of the business you messaged.
  2. Send a WhatsApp message saying delete my data to the business WhatsApp number you used to place an order or chat with the bot.

What happens next

We will take reasonable steps to verify that the request comes from the person whose data is involved. We will then delete or irreversibly anonymise the personal data we hold about you, except where Ghana law requires or permits us to retain it for a limited period (for example, a record of a completed order needed for accounting or dispute handling).

If your data was collected while you were messaging a client business, we will also notify that business of your request so it can take the steps that apply to its own records.
