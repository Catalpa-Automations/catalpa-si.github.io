Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026 (Africa/Accra, GMT)

1. Who we are

This policy is issued by Catalpa SI (also referred to as Catalpa Automations), an enterprise AI and automation business based in Accra, Ghana. In this policy, “Catalpa”, “we”, “us” and “our” mean Catalpa SI.

This policy explains how we collect, use, store and share personal data when you:

  • visit our website at https://catalpa-automations.github.io/catalpa-si.github.io/; or
  • message a WhatsApp ordering bot that we operate for a client business (for example, a shop that takes orders on WhatsApp).

When you chat with a client’s WhatsApp number, you are also dealing with that client business. We process your messages so the bot and the business can take and fulfil your order.

2. Data we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect:

  • your WhatsApp phone number and the name shown on your WhatsApp profile;
  • the content of messages you send to or receive from the bot or the business, including text, voice notes, images, documents and other media;
  • order details, such as products, quantities, prices, delivery or pickup information, and delivery location notes you provide;
  • payment confirmations and related payment status information you send or that we need to confirm an order (for example, a Mobile Money payment screenshot or reference). We do not ask you to send card PINs or Mobile Money PINs, and you should not send them;
  • technical and operational data needed to run the service, such as message timestamps, message IDs, and delivery status; and
  • if you contact us about our own business (for example by email), the contact details and information you send us.

We do not use this website to collect payment card details. We only process personal data that is needed to operate the website, the ordering bots, and related support.

3. Why we use this data

We use personal data to:

  • operate WhatsApp ordering bots for our client businesses;
  • understand and respond to customer messages, including orders, questions, complaints, cancellations and delivery updates;
  • create and manage orders, confirm payments, and share the information the client business needs to fulfil, deliver or follow up on an order;
  • send service messages on behalf of the client business, such as order confirmations or status updates, where those messages are part of the service;
  • improve reliability, fix errors, keep records, and protect the service against abuse or fraud; and
  • respond to privacy, support or legal requests, and meet our legal obligations.

4. Processors and service providers

We use trusted service providers to operate the bots and this website. They process personal data only as needed to provide their services to us. These currently include:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. and its affiliates, which provide the WhatsApp Cloud API and related WhatsApp Business Platform services that carry messages;
  • Vercel, which hosts our applications;
  • the database provider we use to store orders, conversation records and related operational data; and
  • AI model providers we may use to help interpret customer messages and operate the bot. Those providers process message content only to provide that service to us.

WhatsApp itself is a Meta product. Meta’s own terms and privacy policy also apply to your use of WhatsApp.

5. Sharing with the client business

If you message a bot we operate for a client business, we share with that business the personal data needed to take, confirm, fulfil, deliver or support your order. This typically includes your WhatsApp number, name, messages, order and delivery details, and payment confirmations.

The client business is responsible for how it uses that data in its own operations (for example, packing and delivering your order). We do not sell customer lists or personal data.

We may also disclose personal data if we are required to do so by law, or if it is necessary to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the service.

6. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only for as long as it is needed to operate the service, fulfil orders, handle disputes or support requests, and meet legal or accounting obligations. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or irreversibly anonymise it, unless a longer period is required by law.

If a client relationship ends, we disconnect the bot and, on request, provide the client with an export of their customer and order data, as described in our terms with that client. We then delete or anonymise personal data we no longer need.

7. Your rights and how to delete your data

Under Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you have rights in relation to your personal data. These include, where applicable, the right to be informed about how your data is used, to request access to it, to request correction of inaccurate data, and to request deletion or to object to certain processing, subject to any legal exceptions (for example, where we must keep a record of a completed order for a limited period).

To request access, correction or deletion of your data, you can:

  • email p.samuelamoakoh@gmail.com with the subject line “Delete my data” (or “Access my data” / “Correct my data”), and include the WhatsApp number you used and the business you messaged; or
  • send a WhatsApp message saying delete my data to the business WhatsApp number you used to place an order or chat with the bot.

We will take reasonable steps to verify that the request comes from the person whose data is involved, then delete or anonymise the personal data we hold about you, except where we are required or permitted by law to retain it (for example, a limited order record needed for accounting or dispute handling). If the data is also held by the client business, we will also notify that business of your request so it can take the steps that apply to its own records.

A shorter, standalone copy of these deletion instructions is available at data-deletion.html. That page is the URL we provide to Meta as our user data deletion instructions.

8. Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)

We process personal data in line with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of Ghana. We aim to process data lawfully, fairly and for the stated purposes, to keep it accurate and not longer than necessary, and to protect it with appropriate technical and organisational measures.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle a privacy request, you may also have the right to complain to Ghana’s data protection authority, the Data Protection Commission.
