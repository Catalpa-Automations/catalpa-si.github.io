# Client onboarding checklist — WhatsApp Business Ops (DFY)

Use this for every Accra/Kumasi install. **Source of truth for chat = Meta WhatsApp Business.** Our tracker is a thin companion, not a second inbox.

---

## A. Pre-kickoff (before you touch their phone)

- [ ] Deposit received (MoMo/Paystack) — note ref + amount GHS
- [ ] Legal / trading name + preferred display name
- [ ] Logo (PNG/JPG) + brand colours (optional)
- [ ] Business category (pharmacy, fashion, spare parts, clinic…)
- [ ] Address / area (e.g. Osu, Madina, Adum) + Google Maps pin if any
- [ ] Hours (weekday / Saturday / Sunday)
- [ ] Primary number that will run **WhatsApp Business** (not personal forever)
- [ ] Who answers today vs who *should* answer (owner / cashier / junior)
- [ ] List of top 10–30 products or services + prices (GHS)
- [ ] MoMo number or Paystack payment link for deposits/orders
- [ ] Sample FAQs they type every day (price, location, delivery, hours)

---

## B. Meta WhatsApp Business setup (on-device)

Do this **inside Meta’s WhatsApp Business app** (Android/iOS). Do not migrate them to a third-party “WhatsApp CRM inbox” unless they already ask for Cloud API later.

- [ ] Install / open **WhatsApp Business** on the dedicated handset
- [ ] Business profile: name, category, description, address, hours, email/website
- [ ] Profile photo = clear logo or storefront
- [ ] **Away message** + **greeting message** enabled with Ghana-friendly copy
- [ ] **Quick replies** (min 8): welcome, price list, MoMo pay, location, delivery, hours, “we’ll call back”, thank-you
- [ ] **Labels** created to mirror pipeline: `New` · `Contacted` · `Quoted` · `Won` · `Lost` (+ optional `VIP`, `Supplier`)
- [ ] Label colours agreed; staff can apply in 1 tap
- [ ] **Catalog** created OR price-list PDF/image pinned in starred message
- [ ] Catalog items match agreed GHS prices (no “DM for price” on top SKUs)
- [ ] Labels + quick replies tested by sending from a second phone
- [ ] Short note: chats stay in WhatsApp Business / Business Suite — we don’t replace Meta

---

## C. Ghana money + links

- [ ] MoMo / Paystack link tested with GHS 1–2 (or demo link)
- [ ] Quick reply “PAY” includes the live link (not a screenshot-as-proof flow)
- [ ] `wa.me/233…` click-to-chat link generated for flyers / GBP / website
- [ ] Status / broadcast list hygiene explained (no spam; 24h window awareness)

---

## D. Thin lead tracker (companion only)

Choose **one** per client:

- [ ] **Web tracker** (`whatsapp-crm-dfy` app) seeded with sample → renamed for client, OR
- [ ] **Google Sheet** from `sheets/leads-template.csv` with stage column + `wa.me` formula

- [ ] Stages match WhatsApp labels: New → Contacted → Quoted → Won/Lost
- [ ] Staff know: **reply in WhatsApp**; update stage label + tracker after
- [ ] Export CSV shown once (backup / accountant)

---

## E. Staff training (30–45 min, on their phones)

- [ ] Who owns the Business phone overnight / Sundays
- [ ] How to apply labels after every meaningful reply
- [ ] How to send catalog / quick reply / MoMo link
- [ ] When to mark Quoted vs Won/Lost
- [ ] Escalation: when to call the owner
- [ ] SOP one-pager saved to phone Files / printed at till

---

## F. Handover pack

- [ ] SOP PDF / WhatsApp note with screenshots
- [ ] Login/recovery: Google/Apple account on phone documented for owner
- [ ] Tracker URL or Sheet link + who has edit access
- [ ] Retainer offer (GHS/mo) + what is included
- [ ] Day-7 check-in booked
- [ ] Upsell noted: GBP claim, 5-page site, CTWA ads (optional)

---

## G. Out of scope (say this out loud)

- We do **not** rebuild WhatsApp, Instagram, or Meta Business Suite
- We do **not** hold customer funds or accept screenshot-only “proof of MoMo”
- Cloud API / shared team inbox tools are a **later upsell** after Business app is healthy
