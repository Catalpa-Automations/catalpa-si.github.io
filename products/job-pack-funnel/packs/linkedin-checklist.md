# LinkedIn Checklist — Ghana Job Seekers

Use this once. Tick every box before you apply this week.

## Profile photo & banner

- [ ] Clear headshot (plain wall / outdoor light) — no group cutouts
- [ ] Banner: simple Accra skyline, industry image, or solid brand colour — not default blue
- [ ] Name matches your CV (no nickname-only)

## Headline (120 characters)

Formula: **Role you want | Proof skill | City/Open to**

Examples:

- `Graduate Trainee | Excel & Customer Ops | Accra · Open to work`
- `Junior Accountant | QuickBooks · NSS completed | Greater Accra`
- `Sales Executive | B2B / FMCG | Kumasi · MoMo & field sales`

Avoid: `Student at University of Ghana looking for opportunities` (too vague).

## About section (first 3 lines matter)

1. Who you are + target role  
2. One proof point (project, NSS win, campus leadership)  
3. Call to action: “Happy to chat on WhatsApp: +233 …”

## Experience & Featured

- [ ] NSS / internship listed with 2–3 bullets each (verbs + results)
- [ ] Education includes programme + expected/grad year
- [ ] Featured: PDF of CV or one project slide (optional)

## Skills & social proof

- [ ] Pin top 3 skills that match jobs you’re applying for
- [ ] Ask 1 lecturer / supervisor for a recommendation (send them a draft paragraph)
- [ ] Turn on **Open to Work** — visible to recruiters only if you’re employed and discreet

## Weekly 30-minute routine

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Mon | Apply to 5 roles; save posts from 10 target companies |
| Wed | Comment thoughtfully on 3 Accra hiring posts (not “Interested”) |
| Fri | Message 2 alumni: short intro + ask for 10 minutes, not a job |

## Message template (connection + ask)

> Hi [Name], I’m a [programme] graduate targeting [role] in Accra. I admired [company/project]. Would you be open to a short WhatsApp chat about what makes candidates stand out on your team? — [Your name]

## Red flags to avoid

- Fake “CEO / Founder” titles for campus side-hustles without context  
- Skill spam (50 random skills)  
- Copy-pasting USA-only jargon with zero Ghana context  
- Posting salary complaints publicly while job hunting
