### Ghana recruiter tips (delete before sending)

1. Put **phone with WhatsApp** near the top — many shortlists happen on chat.
2. Write **GHS** amounts only if discussing sales targets you hit; don’t invent figures.
3. Match keywords from the Jobberman / LinkedIn ad in your summary.
4. PDF filename: `Ama_Mensah_CV_Graduate_Trainee.pdf` — not `CV_final_final2.pdf`.
