# Interview Prep — Ghana Edition

## Before you go / join Zoom

- [ ] Research company: what they sell, who they serve in Ghana, recent news
- [ ] Print CV + notepad; charge phone; test Zoom name/camera
- [ ] Prepare 1 story for **teamwork**, **pressure**, **mistake**, **initiative** (STAR)

### STAR reminder

**S**ituation → **T**ask → **A**ction (you) → **R**esult (number if possible)

## Common questions + answer frames

### 1. Tell me about yourself

**Frame (60–90 seconds):** Present → Past → Future  

“I’m a [degree] graduate from [uni]. During NSS at [org] I [win]. I’m strongest in [skills]. I’m excited about [this role] because [company fit].”

### 2. Why this company?

Name a real product, branch, or customer segment in Ghana — not “you’re a leading company.”

### 3. What are your salary expectations?

- Research band on Jobberman / peers for that role in Accra.  
- Answer with a **range in GHS**, flexible for benefits:  
  “Based on similar graduate roles in Accra, I’m targeting **GHS X–Y** monthly, open to discuss total package.”

### 4. Strength / weakness

Strength = skill they need + proof.  
Weakness = real but not fatal + what you’re doing about it (e.g. public speaking → Toastmasters / recorded practice).

### 5. Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

Show growth **in their industry**, not “I’ll have left for Canada” (even if true — keep it professional).

## Sector flavour

| Sector | They listen for |
|--------|-----------------|
| Banks / fintech | Integrity, numeracy, customer patience, compliance |
| NGOs | Beneficiary empathy, reporting discipline, donor language |
| Startups | Speed, ownership, WhatsApp/ops hustle, learning curve |
| FMCG / sales | Targets, field energy, MoMo collections discipline |

## Questions you should ask them

1. What does success look like in the first 90 days?  
2. Which tools does the team use daily?  
3. How is feedback given?  
4. Next steps and timeline?

## After the interview (same day)

Send a short thank-you email or WhatsApp if they shared a number:

> Thank you for today’s conversation about the [role]. I enjoyed learning about [specific detail]. I’m very interested and happy to share any extra samples. — [Name]
