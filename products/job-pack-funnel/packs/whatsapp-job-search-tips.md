# WhatsApp Job-Search Tips & Scripts

Most Ghana shortlists move on WhatsApp. Stay professional — treat chat like email with speed.

## Rules

1. Save the recruiter’s name and company in your contacts.  
2. No voice notes for first contact unless they send one first.  
3. Don’t spam “Please I need job” in groups — value + CV link.  
4. Keep files under 5–8 MB; PDF only.

## After applying on Jobberman / LinkedIn

> Good afternoon [Name], I’ve applied for the **[Role]** via [Jobberman/LinkedIn]. I’ve attached my CV for convenience. Happy to share availability for a brief call this week. Thank you. — [Full name], [Phone]

## Following up (5–7 days later)

> Good morning [Name], just a polite follow-up on my application for **[Role]** submitted on [date]. Happy to redo any form or assessment. Thanks for your time.

## Asking an alumni for a referral

> Hi [Name], I’m [Your name], [programme/year] from [uni]. I saw [Company] is hiring for [Role]. If you’re open to it, could you advise whether an internal referral is appropriate — and what the team values most? I can send a 5-line blurb + CV.

## Group etiquette (campus / job groups)

- Read rules pinned at the top.  
- Post once with: role sought · city · 1 proof line · CV link (Drive “anyone with link”).  
- DM people who post openings — don’t argue under posts.

## Red flags

- “Pay GHS X for guaranteed job / NSS placement”  
- Recruiters who refuse company email / office address  
- Requests for MoMo “registration fees” before interview  

If unsure, ask them to send an official offer letter on company letterhead first.
