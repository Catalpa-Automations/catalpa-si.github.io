# Principle: Solution-first commercial

**Company:** Catalpa Automations (Ghana)  
**Companion:** [`docs/catalpa-foundation.md`](../docs/catalpa-foundation.md)  
**Not legal advice** — contracts need a Ghana lawyer review.

## When it applies

- Any **bot / dashboard / CTWA digitization** engagement for a business client.
- Any pitch where Catalpa would otherwise ask for an **upfront development fee**.
- Does **not** force free work on: Meta ad spend, unrelated SKUs (website sprint cash packs, job packs, RatePing subs), or work outside a written pilot scope.

## Decision rule

1. **Show before charge** — Client sees their solution live (demo tenant → live proof on their WhatsApp) before any development invoice.
2. **Time-box the free** — Pilot has a written end date and revision cap. Free ≠ forever.
3. **Pay starts on commitment** — Subscription, per-order, and/or retainer begins only after Live Commitment / Order Form (or when they keep production live past pilot end).
4. **Fair both ways** — No hostage IP (client keeps number + data export); no unpaid infinite build (Catalpa suspends when pilot ends without pay).
5. **Write it down** — Scope, exclusions, ads-spend ownership, and cancel terms in plain language before go-live.

## Stopping boundary

Stop giving free pilot work when **any** of these is true:

- Pilot end date passed and no signed Live Commitment  
- Revision / scope cap exhausted and client wants more without an addendum or paid plan  
- Client demands “free forever,” unlimited custom build, or Catalpa to fund ad spend  
- Client will not provide catalog access / WA access needed for live proof after reasonable chase  
- Continuing would mean Catalpa loses money unfairly (open-ended labor with no convert path)

**Then:** Suspend demo/live tenant with polite notice; offer the Order Form; keep the door open — do not litigate over a free pilot; do not extend free work by oral promise.
