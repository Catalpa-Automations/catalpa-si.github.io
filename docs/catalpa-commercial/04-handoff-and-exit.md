## 1. Promise (both ways)

When the relationship ends — after pilot or after paid service:

- Client must be able to **leave with their customer and order data**.  
- Catalpa must be able to **keep its bot software, platform, and templates**.  
- Nobody holds the other’s property to force a payment fight. Money disputes are handled with invoices, reminders, and the written agreement — **not** by deleting Client’s only copy of their buyers list or by Client copying Catalpa’s source.

---

## 2. What Client gets on exit

Within **_____ business days** after disconnect date (default **5**), Catalpa will provide, on request:

| Export | Format (typical) | Includes |
|--------|------------------|----------|
| Customers / leads | CSV | Name, phone, tags/status if stored |
| Orders | CSV | Order id, items, amounts, payment status, timestamps |
| Simple notes / statuses | CSV or PDF summary | What was on the dashboard |

- Delivery: WhatsApp file, email, or secure link — Client chooses a practical method.  
- Catalpa may keep **backups** for a short period (e.g. up to **30 days**) for dispute/safety, then delete or anonymise operational copies per Catalpa’s retention practice — unless law requires longer.  
- Catalpa does **not** keep using Client’s customer list to sell for other shops.

**Client already owns** their Meta / WhatsApp Business assets, creatives they paid for, and brand files they supplied.

---

## 3. Bot disconnect (clean cut)

On or before the disconnect date, Catalpa will:

- [ ] Switch off or disconnect the Catalpa-hosted bot from Client’s WhatsApp webhook / integration  
- [ ] Revoke Client users’ access to Catalpa’s hosted dashboard (after export window if still needed)  
- [ ] Confirm in WhatsApp/email: “Bot disconnected on ______”  

Client should:

- [ ] Pause or reassign Meta Ads that point at the old bot flow  
- [ ] Update wa.me / website CTAs if they no longer want that number’s automated flow  
- [ ] Remove Catalpa staff from Client’s Business Manager if access was granted  

Catalpa will **not**:

- Demand an “exit fee” to release Client’s own data  
- Refuse export solely because a bill is disputed (unpaid **platform** fees may still be owed — collect them normally; still release data)  
- Wipe Client’s Meta account (Catalpa should not have that power; if admin access was shared, return/remove it)

---

## 4. What Catalpa keeps (platform IP)

Catalpa retains all rights to:

- Bot code, flow designs, prompts, platform infrastructure  
- Dashboard software, internal tools, playbooks, templates  
- Know-how and future product improvements  

End of service = end of Client’s **licence to use** the hosted Catalpa system. It is **not** a transfer of Catalpa’s IP. Client must not scrape, clone, or resell Catalpa’s platform.

---

## 5. Optional paid transition help

If Client wants help moving to another vendor or to manual WhatsApp only, Catalpa may offer a **short paid handoff** (quoted in GHS). This is optional — export + disconnect above do **not** depend on buying extra help.
