## 1. What this pilot is

Catalpa SI (“**Catalpa**”) will set up a **live WhatsApp bot and a simple dashboard** so Client can see how Click-to-WhatsApp ads → chat → orders → dispatch can work for their shop.

This is a **short trial**. It is **not** a long-term contract and **not** a promise that ads will make profit.

---

## 2. What is free (Catalpa side)

During the pilot, Catalpa does **not** charge for:

- Building / configuring the demo bot for the agreed scope  
- Access to the pilot dashboard  
- Reasonable setup help so Client can try the flow  

**Client pays Catalpa GHS 0 for development during this pilot.**

---

## 3. What Client pays (always Client’s money)

Client pays **directly** (not through Catalpa’s pocket unless written otherwise):

| Item | Who pays |
|------|----------|
| **Meta Ads / Click-to-WhatsApp ad spend** | **Client** |
| Meta / WhatsApp Business or Cloud API fees (if any) | **Client** |
| Client’s own MoMo / Paystack / bank charges | **Client** |
| Internet, phones, staff time | **Client** |

Catalpa will not run large ad budgets on Catalpa’s card and then surprise-bill Client. Ad accounts and spend stay under **Client’s** control unless both sides write a different arrangement.

---

## 5. How long / how it ends

1. Pilot runs from **start date** to **end date** above (default **7–14 days**).  
2. Either party may **stop the pilot early** by WhatsApp or email notice to the other side.  
3. When the pilot ends (or stops early):  
   - Catalpa may disconnect the pilot bot  
   - Client may request an export of **their** customer / order data collected in the pilot (see handoff doc)  
4. If Client wants to continue on a paid plan, both sides sign **02-live-commitment.md** and Client pays the first fee **before** full production service continues.

**One free pilot per business** unless Catalpa agrees in writing to extend. Extensions are a courtesy, not a right.

---

## 6. Who owns what

| Thing | Owner |
|-------|--------|
| Bot software, flows Catalpa built, platform, dashboards, templates, know-how | **Catalpa** |
| Client’s customer names, phone numbers, chat content about Client’s buyers, order records of Client’s sales | **Client** |
| Client’s trademarks, logos, product photos Client supplied | **Client** |
| Meta / WhatsApp assets in Client’s Business Manager | **Client** (per Meta’s rules) |

Client does **not** get Catalpa source code, admin keys to Catalpa’s platform, or a right to copy the bot for another vendor during or after the pilot.

Catalpa does **not** sell, spam, or reuse Client’s customer list for other businesses.

---

## 7. No guarantee of ad results (ROAS)

Ads depend on offer, creative, price, stock, and market luck.

- Catalpa does **not** guarantee clicks, leads, sales, or return on ad spend (**ROAS**).  
- Any sample numbers in chats or proposals are **examples**, not promises.  
- Client decides their own daily/weekly ad budget and can pause ads anytime.

---

## 8. Behaviour during the pilot (fair use)

Client agrees to:

- Give truthful catalog / price / stock info  
- Not demand large out-of-scope builds “while it’s free”  
- Not share Catalpa dashboard logins with competitors  

Catalpa agrees to:

- Act respectfully and keep Client data reasonably safe  
- Be clear if something is outside scope  
- Not invent fees for the pilot build  

If Client uses the pilot mainly to extract free custom software outside the scope list, Catalpa may end the pilot immediately.

---

## 9. After the pilot — paths

| Path | What happens |
|------|----------------|

| **Stop** | Bot disconnect; Client data export on request; no Catalpa development invoice for the pilot |
| **Pause & decide later** | Bot may be switched off; a new pilot is not automatic |
