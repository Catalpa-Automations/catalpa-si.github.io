## B. Out of scope (unless a separate paid quote)

These are **not** included in the standard pilot or SaaS spine. Asking for them mid-project does **not** make them free.

| Out of scope | Why we call it out |
|--------------|--------------------|
| Custom **ERP / accounting** (e.g. full Sage, Tally, or bespoke ledgers) | Different product; needs its own project |
| **Native mobile apps** (iOS / Android stores) | Not the WhatsApp spine |
| Building a **new chat network** or WhatsApp clone | We ride Meta; we don’t replace it |
| Full **e-commerce website** with cart SEO / multi-vendor marketplace | Use website sprint or separate quote |
| **Unlimited** custom bot branches, languages, or integrations | Scope must be listed |
| Connecting every random tool (unlisted POS, banks, SMS gateways) | Each connector = new quote |
| **Guaranteed** ad ROAS, sales, or bank loan outcomes | Nobody honest can promise this |
| 24/7 human call-centre staffing by Catalpa | Bot ≠ full contact centre unless quoted |
| Migrating years of old chats from personal phones without limits | Agree volume first |
| Legal / tax / licensing filings for Client’s business | Client’s lawyer / accountant |
| Buying stock, delivering parcels, or collecting cash in the street | Client’s operations |
| Paying Meta invoices or ad spend from Catalpa’s pocket | Client’s billing |

## C. Grey area rule (street-practical)

If Client asks for something that sounds small but is not on list **A**:

1. Catalpa says whether it fits remaining pilot/paid support time.  
2. If not, Catalpa sends a **change quote** (see Live Commitment).  
3. No work on that item until Client accepts the quote.

This protects Client from surprise bills and protects Catalpa from free-work abuse.
