# Selling ad script — catalpa-ctwa-spine

**Length target:** ≤78s  
**Audience:** Ghana SME owner (chop bar, boutique, clinic, spare parts)  
**Visual setup:** Phone screen-record WhatsApp bot + laptop/tablet dashboard. Simple VO, calm pace.

---

## Hook (0–8s)

**VO:** “Your ads send buyers to WhatsApp. Then… silence. Orders die while you sleep.”

**On screen:** Meta-style “Click to WhatsApp” ad → chat opens → unread bubbles stacking. Cut to tired shop owner looking at phone.

---

## Problem (8–20s)

**VO:** “You can’t reply all day. Customers leave. You don’t know who paid. Morning comes — no list of what sold.”

**On screen:** Missed messages. Someone typing “I sent MoMo” with a blurry screenshot. Empty or messy notes notebook.

---

## Demo flow (20–60s)

**VO:** “Catalpa’s WhatsApp bot takes the order for you — twenty-four seven.”

**On screen (real UI):** Buyer taps ad → WhatsApp greets with shop name → picks from menu → cart confirms.

**VO:** “They pay with MoMo. The system marks it paid — not a screenshot.”

**On screen:** Paystack / MoMo pay page → success → order flips to **Paid** on dashboard.

**VO:** “Your dashboard shows every lead, every paid order. One tap sends it to kitchen or rider.”

**On screen:** Owner dashboard: new order row → Paid → Dispatch / rider WhatsApp handoff.

*(Aim ~40s on this beat; keep cuts tight.)*

---

## Benefit + CTA (60–78s)

**VO:** “Ads bring the click. Catalpa turns it into a paid order you can see — even when you’re offline.”

**On screen:** Split: sleeping owner / phone on charge · dashboard with green Paid + dispatched.

**VO:** “Want it on your number? We’ll show you a live demo for your shop — free until you see it work.”

**On screen end card:** **Catalpa SI** · WhatsApp CTA / contact · product name: *Orders from WhatsApp ads*

---

## Shot checklist

- [ ] Real WhatsApp bot thread (demo tenant OK)
- [ ] MoMo / Paystack pay step
- [ ] Dashboard Paid + dispatch
- [ ] One outcome line on end card
- [ ] Total runtime ≤78s
